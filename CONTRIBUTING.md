# Contributing to VorzixDev Projects

Thank you for your interest in contributing to VorzixDev.

## 1. Before Contributing

1. Confirm that the repository accepts external contributions.
2. Review the repository README, licence, security policy, and issue tracker.
3. Discuss substantial changes before implementing them.
4. Do not begin confidential or commercial work without written project terms and access approval.

An unsolicited contribution may be reviewed, modified, declined, or closed. Submission or acceptance does not automatically create employment, compensation, revenue-share, ownership, or continuing-maintenance rights.

## 2. Intellectual Property and Commercial Contributions

VorzixDev does not accept substantial unsolicited code, assets, audio, models, artwork, animation, documentation, or other copyrightable production work for commercial products unless separate written contribution terms have been accepted.

Opening an issue, submitting an idea, reporting a bug, or discussing an improvement does not transfer ownership of the submitter's original work.

A contributor's copyright and VorzixDev's permission to use, modify, distribute, sublicense, sell, or include a contribution in a commercial product are separate matters and must not be assumed.

Before submitting substantial work, contact VorzixDev to determine whether a Project-Specific Agreement, Contributor Licence Agreement, copyright assignment, commercial licence, repository licence, or other written authorization is required.

Do not submit commercial production work unless ownership, modification, distribution, sublicensing, customer-use, withdrawal, attribution, and source-file terms have been confirmed in writing.

Unless separate written terms state otherwise:

- VorzixDev does not claim ownership merely because material was discussed or submitted.
- VorzixDev is not required to use, publish, distribute, purchase, or accept a submission.
- A merged pull request confirms repository acceptance of the change but does not by itself transfer copyright or create commercial rights beyond the applicable repository licence and separately accepted terms.
- VorzixDev may decline substantial external contributions until appropriate written intellectual-property terms are available.

## 3. Originality, Licensing, and Provenance

Contributors must:

- Submit only work they created or are authorized to use.
- Identify third-party code, assets, fonts, audio, models, textures, plugins, libraries, datasets, and templates.
- Preserve required notices and attribution.
- Disclose conditions affecting commercial use, modification, sublicensing, distribution, or redistribution.
- Disclose material AI assistance when it affects provenance, licensing, security, accuracy, or review.
- Keep source links, creator names, licence names, download dates, and required attribution for approved external materials.

VorzixDev may request original source files, project files, dependencies, licences, creation evidence, or written confirmation of authorship and authorization.

## 4. Development Workflow

Unless a repository says otherwise:

1. Create or reference an approved issue.
2. Work in a separate branch.
3. Keep each pull request focused on one purpose.
4. Use clear commit messages.
5. Add or update tests and documentation where appropriate.
6. Complete the pull-request checklist.
7. Address review comments before requesting final approval.

Do not commit secrets, credentials, tokens, private customer data, payment data, confidential agreements, or unreleased commercial assets to a public repository.

## 5. Code and Asset Quality

Code should be readable, modular, documented where appropriate, safe with untrusted input, and free from hidden telemetry, external loaders, backdoors, credential collection, and unnecessary obfuscation.

Non-code deliverables should include, where applicable, editable source files, production exports, clear naming, folder structure, licence records, technical integration notes, preview files, and reasonable revisions.

## 6. Testing and Evidence

A contribution may require reproduction steps, expected and actual results, platform and version details, screenshots or video, sanitized logs, test cases, and retest results.

Only work recorded as accepted through the applicable review process is treated as an accepted project contribution.

## 7. Review and Acceptance

VorzixDev may mark a submission as Accepted, Accepted with Follow-up Work, Pending Revision, Declined, or Out of Scope.

Pull-request approval confirms repository acceptance of that change. It does not by itself modify compensation, revenue-share, copyright ownership, commercial-use permission, sublicensing rights, attribution terms, or maintenance obligations.

## 8. Security Reports

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, leaked secret, exploit, or access-control problem. Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 9. Questions

For contribution and intellectual-property questions, contact **vorzixdev.official@gmail.com** before submitting substantial work.

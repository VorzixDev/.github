# VorzixDev Project Governance

## 1. Purpose

This document explains the public operational structure used to manage VorzixDev repositories and projects. It does not publish private commercial terms, contributor percentages, personal records, or confidential agreements.

## 2. Roles

### Organization Owner

Controls organization-level security, membership, repository ownership, sensitive settings, and final access decisions.

### Product or Project Lead

Defines the approved project scope, milestones, review process, integration plan, and release readiness for an assigned project.

### Maintainer or Reviewer

Reviews issues, pull requests, assets, tests, and documentation within assigned permissions. Maintainer status does not automatically grant organization ownership, financial access, or authority to alter commercial terms.

### Contributor

Performs approved work within a defined scope. A contributor may be project-based, paid, revenue-share, hybrid, reward-based, or voluntary, depending on separately accepted terms.

### Volunteer QA Tester

Participates voluntarily in approved testing assignments. Unless a separate written agreement states otherwise, this role is unpaid and does not receive revenue share. Testers may receive product credit, a verified testing record, or consideration for future opportunities. Credit is not a substitute for compensation where compensation was promised.

### Community Moderator

Manages community conduct in assigned spaces. Moderation does not automatically grant repository, financial, code, customer-data, or organization-owner access.

### Administrator

Receives only specifically approved administrative permissions. Administrator access is not awarded automatically through an application and should follow trust, need, and least-privilege review.

## 3. Decision Authority

Only authorized project leadership may:

- Approve or expand project scope
- Assign production work
- Accept final deliverables
- Approve releases
- Change repository access
- Accept commercial dependencies
- Propose compensation or revenue-share changes

Private financial and contractual changes require the applicable written acceptance and cannot be created through a public issue, pull request, code commit, Discord role, or informal statement.

## 4. Access Control

VorzixDev follows least-privilege principles:

- Access is limited to the repositories and permissions required for assigned work.
- Organization-owner access is restricted.
- Contributors should not share accounts or credentials.
- Access may be reviewed, reduced, or removed when responsibilities change.
- Confidential work should remain in approved private repositories and channels.

## 5. Contribution Acceptance

Work is not accepted merely because it was started, uploaded, committed, or demonstrated.

Acceptance requires the applicable scope, submission method, review, and recorded decision. Project-specific acceptance criteria may override this public default.

## 6. Compensation Classification

Where applicable, a project-specific agreement should classify a role as one of the following:

- Fixed fee
- Revenue share
- Hybrid compensation
- Reward-based participation
- Voluntary unpaid participation

Public GitHub activity does not by itself create compensation rights.

## 7. Volunteer QA

Volunteer QA participation must be clearly presented as optional and unpaid before a testing assignment is accepted.

Volunteer testers:

- May decline or leave an assignment before acceptance of additional work
- Must follow confidentiality and responsible-disclosure rules
- Must not publicly disclose unreleased builds or vulnerabilities
- Should receive clear test scope and reporting instructions
- May be credited where appropriate and consented to

## 8. Conflicts and Appeals

Project concerns should first be raised privately with the assigned project lead. Security concerns follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Conduct concerns follow [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

VorzixDev may document decisions, request additional evidence, assign another reviewer, or escalate the matter to organization ownership.

## 9. Document Hierarchy

When documents conflict, the following order generally applies:

1. Applicable law and platform requirements
2. Valid project-specific written agreements
3. Product or repository-specific terms and licences
4. VorzixDev internal contributor and finance policies
5. This public governance document
6. General repository guidance

This public document does not override legally binding or privately accepted terms.

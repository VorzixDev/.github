# VorzixDev Product Support Guide

## Where to Request Support

For general product support, setup assistance, documentation questions, and reproducible technical problems, use the official support channel listed with the product or contact **vorzixdev.official@gmail.com**.

Purchase-specific questions may also be submitted through the messaging or support system of the storefront used for the transaction.

For public repository bugs and feature requests, use the relevant issue form when public reporting is appropriate.

Security vulnerabilities, leaked secrets, and exploit details must be reported privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Include This Information

- Product or repository name
- Product version or release
- Engine, platform, and relevant version
- Device and operating system
- Clear reproduction steps
- Expected and actual behavior
- Sanitized error messages or logs
- Screenshots or video when helpful
- Changes made before the problem began

## Do Not Send

Do not send passwords, cookies, recovery codes, authentication tokens, bot tokens, private keys, API keys, payment credentials, full customer records, government identification, home addresses, or unauthorized confidential files.

## Support Boundaries

Support scope depends on the product page, licence, documentation, version, purchase terms, and support status. Support may exclude unrelated custom development, heavily modified customer code, pirated or redistributed copies, unsupported platforms or versions, conflicts caused by unrelated third-party products, training, consulting, and project management unless separately agreed.

## Feature Requests

Feature requests may be reviewed, modified, combined, postponed, declined, or accepted. A suggestion does not create ownership, payment, revenue-share, contributor status, or a guarantee of implementation.

## Refunds and Purchases

Purchase, licence, tax, refund, and storefront questions are handled under the terms and processes of the storefront used for the transaction and the product's applicable terms.

# VorzixDev Product Support Guide

## 1. Where to Request Support

For product and account-independent technical support, use the official support channel listed with the product or contact **vorzixdev.official@gmail.com**.

For GitHub repository bugs and feature requests, use the relevant issue form when public reporting is appropriate.

Security vulnerabilities, leaked secrets, and exploit details must be reported privately under [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## 2. Include This Information

A useful support request should include:

- Product or repository name
- Product version or release
- Engine, platform, and relevant version
- Device and operating system
- Clear reproduction steps
- Expected and actual behavior
- Error messages or logs with sensitive information removed
- Screenshots or video when helpful
- Changes made before the problem began

## 3. Do Not Send

Do not send:

- Passwords, session cookies, recovery codes, or authentication tokens
- Bot tokens, private keys, API keys, or payment credentials
- Full customer or player records
- Government identification or home addresses
- Confidential third-party files without authorization

## 4. Support Boundaries

Support scope depends on the applicable product page, licence, documentation, version, and purchase terms.

Support may not include:

- Building an unrelated custom system
- Rewriting heavily modified customer code
- Supporting pirated, leaked, or redistributed copies
- Maintaining unsupported engines, platforms, plugins, or versions
- Fixing conflicts caused by unrelated third-party products
- Training, consulting, or project management unless separately agreed

## 5. Feature Requests

Feature requests may be reviewed, modified, combined, postponed, declined, or accepted. A suggestion does not create ownership, payment, revenue-share, contributor status, or a guarantee of implementation.

## 6. Response Expectations

VorzixDev does not guarantee an immediate response through this public guide. Priority may depend on security impact, product status, severity, reproducibility, available evidence, and existing support terms.

## 7. Refunds and Purchases

Purchase, licence, tax, refund, and storefront questions are handled under the terms and processes of the storefront used for the transaction and the product's applicable terms.

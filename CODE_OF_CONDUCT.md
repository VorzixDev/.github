# VorzixDev Community and Contributor Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

VorzixDev is committed to maintaining professional, constructive, and secure spaces for contributors, customers, community members, testers, and collaborators.

This Code applies to VorzixDev repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project channels, review sessions, support spaces, and other official collaboration environments.

## 2. Expected Conduct

Participants must:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Give actionable, project-focused feedback.
- Disagree with ideas without attacking people.
- Respect project scope, deadlines, review decisions, and access limits.
- Protect confidential, private, and security-sensitive information.
- Credit the actual creators of code, art, models, audio, documentation, and other work.
- Disclose relevant third-party, licensed, open-source, team-created, template-based, or AI-assisted materials.
- Report mistakes, blockers, conflicts of interest, and security concerns promptly.

## 3. Unacceptable Conduct

The following are not permitted:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, targeted abuse, or discriminatory conduct.
- Spam, repeated mentions, pressure, or aggressive demands.
- Impersonating VorzixDev staff, contributors, customers, or partners.
- False portfolio claims or presenting another person's work as your own.
- Sharing passwords, session tokens, cookies, recovery codes, payment credentials, private keys, or confidential files.
- Publishing unreleased assets, source code, financial information, agreements, customer information, or internal discussions without authorization.
- Introducing malicious code, backdoors, credential collectors, destructive behavior, or undisclosed telemetry.
- Using project channels for unauthorized advertising, solicitation, or unrelated promotion.
- Publicly disclosing a vulnerability before VorzixDev has had a reasonable opportunity to investigate and address it.

## 4. Project Communication

Development updates, review requests, bug reports, corrections, and technical disagreements must remain professional.

Individual compensation, revenue-share, private agreements, personal information, and access disputes should be handled only in approved private channels.

No participant may create a new task, expand a role, or claim a higher allocation merely by beginning unapproved work. Scope and compensation changes require written approval before the additional work begins.

## 5. Reporting Conduct Issues

Report conduct concerns privately to **vorzixdev.official@gmail.com**.

Include:

- A concise description of the incident
- Relevant repository, channel, issue, or pull-request links
- Dates and approximate times
- Screenshots or logs when appropriate
- Any immediate safety or access concern

Do not include passwords, government identification, payment credentials, or unrelated personal information.

## 6. Enforcement

VorzixDev may respond with:

1. Informal guidance or clarification
2. A documented warning
3. Removal of content or access
4. Temporary suspension from project or community spaces
5. Permanent removal from VorzixDev spaces
6. Escalation to the relevant platform or authorities when required

Actions depend on severity, evidence, risk, prior conduct, and applicable agreements. Good-faith reporting, professional disagreement, or raising a legitimate concern is not itself misconduct.

## 7. Scope of This Document

This Code does not replace product licences, project-specific agreements, contributor policies, platform terms, or applicable law. Private commercial terms are maintained separately.

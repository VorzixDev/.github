# VorzixDev Community and Contributor Code of Conduct

## Purpose

VorzixDev is committed to maintaining professional, constructive, and secure spaces for contributors, customers, testers, collaborators, and community members.

## Expected Conduct

Participants must:

- Communicate clearly and respectfully.
- Give actionable, project-focused feedback.
- Respect project scope, deadlines, review decisions, and access limits.
- Protect confidential, private, and security-sensitive information.
- Credit the actual creators of code, art, models, audio, documentation, and other work.
- Disclose relevant third-party, licensed, open-source, team-created, template-based, or AI-assisted materials.
- Report blockers, mistakes, conflicts of interest, and security concerns promptly.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following are not permitted:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, targeted abuse, or discriminatory conduct.
- Spam, repeated mentions, pressure, or aggressive demands.
- Impersonating VorzixDev staff, contributors, customers, or partners.
- False portfolio claims or presenting another person's work as your own.
- Sharing passwords, session tokens, cookies, recovery codes, payment credentials, private keys, or confidential files.
- Publishing unreleased assets, source code, financial information, agreements, customer information, or internal discussions without authorization.
- Introducing malicious code, backdoors, credential collectors, destructive behavior, or undisclosed telemetry.
- Publicly disclosing a vulnerability before coordinated review.

## Project Communication

Individual compensation, revenue-share, private agreements, personal information, and access disputes must be handled only in approved private channels.

No participant may create a new task, expand a role, or claim a higher allocation merely by beginning unapproved work. Scope and compensation changes require written approval before additional work begins.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **vorzixdev.official@gmail.com** with relevant links, dates, evidence, and a concise description. Do not include passwords, government identification, payment credentials, or unrelated personal information.

## Enforcement

VorzixDev may use guidance, warnings, content removal, access restriction, temporary suspension, permanent removal, or platform escalation depending on severity, evidence, risk, prior conduct, and applicable agreements.

## Review and Appeal

A person affected by a moderation or access decision may submit one written request for reconsideration with relevant evidence. Where practical, a different authorized reviewer may assess the request. Reconsideration does not guarantee reversal and does not suspend urgent security restrictions.

## Scope

This Code does not replace product licences, project-specific agreements, contributor policies, platform terms, or applicable law.
